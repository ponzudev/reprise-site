# How to do repeating, with pauses inserted automatically

<p class="lead">Repeating means listening to one sentence (or one phrase) and saying the same sentence out loud while the audio is stopped. It is easier than shadowing, where you keep following the audio, so it is a good way to start practicing out loud.</p>

## The steps of repeating

1. Listen to one sentence. Don't look at the text; focus on the sound and the meaning
2. Say the same sentence while the audio is stopped. Imitate the speed and intonation exactly as you heard them
3. If you can't say it, listen again. If it is still hard, check the text
4. Once you can say it, move on to the next sentence

## What gets in the way with an ordinary player

Repeating means pausing after every sentence, pressing play when you finish speaking, and rewinding a little when you couldn't say it. Doing this every time distracts you, and the actual speaking practice falls by the wayside.

## Repeating with Reprise

Reprise splits audio into sentences automatically, so you don't have to decide where to stop.

1. Set the playback style to “Pause After Each Sentence”
2. When a sentence ends, a cue tone plays and a pause matched to the length of that sentence follows. Say the sentence during the pause
3. When the pause ends, it moves on to the next sentence automatically. To keep practicing the same sentence, switch to “Loop 1 Sentence”

You can change the length of the pause from “…” on the control panel.

- Same as Phrase: waits as long as the sentence you heard. If you have changed the speed, it matches the length you actually heard
- Set Seconds: always waits the same number of seconds

If the next sentence starts before you finish speaking, increase “Cue Tone Timing” in Playback Settings. If a sentence is too long to remember, choose “⋯” → “Split Again” and pick “Phrases” or “Chunks” to split it more finely.

## Once you get used to it

- Hide the text with the eye button and try speaking without looking at it
- Raise the speed (up to 2.0×)
- Move on to [shadowing](/en/guides/shadowing), where you follow the audio without pauses

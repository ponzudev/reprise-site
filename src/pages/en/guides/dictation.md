# How to do dictation with your own audio

<p class="lead">Dictation means writing down exactly what you hear. It shows you clearly which sounds you aren't catching.</p>

## The steps of dictation

1. Listen to one sentence. Divide long audio into pieces of one sentence or shorter
2. Write it down on paper or in a notes app, exactly as you heard it
3. Listen again a few times to the parts you couldn't write
4. Check your answer. Compare it with the text and find out why you missed something (an unknown word, sounds linking together, speed)
5. Finally, read the text aloud. It makes the audio easier to catch the next time you hear it

## Dictation with Reprise

Reprise has no screen for typing in what you write down. Use it together with paper or a notes app.

1. Import audio and split it into sentences
2. Hide the text with the eye button at the left end of the control panel
3. Set the playback style to “Loop 1 Sentence.” The same sentence repeats with a pause in between. Write it down as you listen
4. When you have written it, touch and hold the eye button. The text appears only while you hold it, so you can check your answer on the spot
5. Move on with “Next Sentence”

If a sentence is too long to write down, choose “⋯” → “Split Again” and pick “Phrases” or “Chunks” to split it more finely. If it is too fast, you can slow it down to 0.5×.

## Things to keep in mind

The text is transcribed by speech recognition on your iPhone, so it can contain mistakes. If the answer looks wrong, listen again to check, then touch and hold the sentence to fix the text. If you want to practice with an accurate text, import the audio of study materials that come with a script, or make audio from a text with “Create from Text.”

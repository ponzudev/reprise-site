# How to shadow with any audio you like

<p class="lead">Shadowing means following audio as it plays, without stopping it, and speaking along a moment behind, like a shadow. This page covers the basic steps, and how to practice with audio you like rather than with dedicated study materials.</p>

## The basic steps of shadowing

If you start chasing the audio right away, your mouth often can't keep up. Going through these steps in order makes it easier.

1. First, listen all the way through without looking at the text, and see how much you can catch
2. Check the text. Read the parts you couldn't catch, and look up any words you don't know
3. Speak along with the audio while looking at the text, reading at the same time as the speaker ([overlapping](/en/guides/overlapping))
4. Follow the audio without the text. Hide the text and imitate what you hear, a moment behind. This is shadowing
5. Redo only the sentences you stumbled on. Go back to the sentences you couldn't say and repeat them at a slower speed

It is easier to keep going if you practice a short piece of 1–2 minutes many times. If it feels too fast, start at a lower speed.

## What gets in the way when you use your own audio

Shadowing materials come with a script and breaks that make practice easy. The podcasts you usually listen to, or recordings of your lessons, don't.

- You want to go back to the one sentence you couldn't say, but you can't find its start with the seek bar
- There is no text, so you can't check the sounds you missed
- Even when it is too fast, an ordinary player won't let you fine-tune the speed

## Shadowing with Reprise

Reprise is a language-learning player that transcribes the audio you import on your iPhone and automatically splits it into sentences. You can go through the steps above on a single screen.

1. Import audio. Tap “+” and choose a podcast, a file, a recording or pasted text. Once imported, it is split into sentences automatically
2. Set the playback style to “Play Through” and listen all the way through. The eye button at the left end of the control panel hides the text
3. Check the text. The text appears only while you touch and hold the eye button. Tap a sentence you couldn't catch to hear it again from the start
4. Follow the audio. Adjust the speed between 0.5× and 2.0× (the pitch doesn't change). Try around 0.8× to begin with
5. Repeat the sentences you stumbled on. Set the playback style to “Loop 1 Sentence” to repeat just that sentence. Swipe a sentence to the right to bookmark it, and you can review your bookmarks together later (review requires Reprise Premium)

If a sentence is too long to follow, choose “⋯” → “Split Again” and pick “Phrases” or “Chunks” to split it more finely.

## FAQ

### Does it work with audio that has no script?

Yes. The transcript is made by speech recognition on your iPhone. It can make mistakes, so you can touch and hold a sentence to fix its text.

### Does it work with languages other than English?

It supports English (US, UK and Australia), Japanese, Chinese (Simplified), Korean, French, German and Spanish.

### Can I check how my own voice sounds?

Touch and hold a sentence and choose “Clarity Check.” It records you reading that sentence, and shows word by word how on-device speech recognition heard you. Free users can use it on any 3 sentences per track (every sentence with Reprise Premium). Recording stops automatically when you finish speaking and moves on to the next round, so you can keep practicing. It is not a pronunciation score; it is a guide to whether your words came across as intended.

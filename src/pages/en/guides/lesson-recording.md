# How to review recordings of online lessons and classes, sentence by sentence

<p class="lead">The expressions your teacher rephrased for you in a lesson, and the exchanges where you couldn't say what you wanted, make study material that is yours alone. Listening to the recording again is good review, but finding the one sentence you want in a long recording takes effort.</p>

## Before you record

When you record a lesson or class, **check your teacher's or school's rules, and ask for permission if needed**. Use the recording only for your own study.

## Importing a recording into Reprise

There are three ways.

- Record in Reprise: tap “+” → “Record” to record what's happening around you and turn it into a material
- Send it from Voice Memos: choose “Reprise” in the share menu of Voice Memos
- Import a file: put a file you recorded (audio or video) on a computer into the Files app, then choose it with “+” → “From Files (Audio or Video).” Only the audio is imported from a video

Once imported, it is transcribed on your iPhone and split into sentences.

## How to review

1. Listen all the way through and bookmark the expressions you want to use. Swipe a sentence to the right at your teacher's phrasing or at the places where you couldn't say something
2. Replay one sentence at a time. Tap a sentence to play from its start
3. Speak out loud. With “Pause After Each Sentence,” a pause follows each sentence. Try saying your teacher's sentences after them ([repeating](/en/guides/repeating))
4. Review only the sentences you bookmarked. With “Review Bookmarked Sentences” on the Materials screen, you can practice them together from all your materials (Reprise Premium)

## Good to know

- The audio never leaves your device. Transcription and sentence splitting both happen on your iPhone, and recordings and transcripts are never sent to a server. No account is needed
- Speech recognition works in one language at a time. In a lesson that mixes the language you're learning with your own language, if you set “Recognition Language” to the language you're learning, the parts in your own language won't be transcribed correctly. Touch and hold a sentence and choose “Recognize Again” to recognize just that sentence in a different language
- Noisy recordings, or parts where two people talk at the same time, may not be split well. Touch and hold a sentence to merge it, split it again or adjust its boundaries

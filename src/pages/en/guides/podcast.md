# How to practice with podcasts, replaying one sentence at a time

<p class="lead">Podcasts are easy audio to stick with: you can pick topics that interest you, and new episodes keep coming. But if you just let them play, the parts you can't catch slip past and stay that way.</p>

## What gets in the way when you practice with podcasts

- Going back to the one sentence you missed with the 15-second rewind goes too far or not far enough
- Many shows don't publish a script
- Episodes are long, and you lose track of which part you practiced

## Importing a podcast into Reprise

1. On the Materials screen, tap “+” and choose “From Podcasts”
2. Search by show name. You can also paste an RSS feed URL or an Apple Podcasts share link
3. Choose episodes and import them. Each show becomes one material
4. Open an imported episode and it is transcribed on your iPhone and split into sentences

Now you can tap any sentence to hear it again from the start.

## How to practice

- First, listen all the way through. Hide the text and listen as if you were marking the sentences you can't catch. Swipe a sentence that catches your attention to the right to bookmark it
- Check the sentences you missed. Touch and hold the eye button to see the text. Use “Loop 1 Sentence” to repeat a sentence until you can catch it
- Speak out loud. With “Pause After Each Sentence,” a pause follows each sentence, which you can use for [repeating](/en/guides/repeating) or [shadowing](/en/guides/shadowing)
- Match the speed. For fast shows, start around 0.8×. The pitch doesn't change

You don't need to practice an entire long episode. Repeating just the first 1–2 minutes is an easy way to keep going.

## Good to know

- The transcript comes from speech recognition, so names and fast speech may come out wrong. Touch and hold a sentence to fix it
- Parts with a lot of music or sound effects, or where several people talk at once, may not be split well
- Speech recognition works in one language at a time. Shows spoken only in the language you're learning work better than shows that mix it with explanations in another language
- Shows that don't publish an RSS feed (such as shows exclusive to a streaming service) can't be imported
- Importing new episodes automatically is a Reprise Premium feature. Importing by hand is free

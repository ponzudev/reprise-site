# Reprise Support

Reprise is a language-learning audio player that splits audio into sentences so you can replay and repeat one sentence at a time.
Everything runs on your device, and no account is required.

The names of buttons and menus on this page are the ones the app shows in English. Reprise is available in 14 display languages (Settings → Display).

## Contact

reprise.app.support@gmail.com

If you are reporting a problem, these details help us answer faster:

- Your iPhone model and iOS version
- What you did, and what happened
- The format (mp3, m4a, etc.) and length of the audio you imported

## Requirements

- iPhone with iOS 26 or later

## FAQ

### What is the built-in material “How to Use Reprise”?

It is a tutorial added the first time you open the app. Three tracks (about 4 minutes in total) walk you through the features by voice, in your display language, and you can try each step as you listen. The voice is synthetic.

- **Listen and Repeat, Sentence by Sentence** — tapping a sentence, the silence (pause) after each sentence and “Pause Length,” the playback styles (Play Through / Pause After Each Sentence / Loop 1 Sentence), speed, hiding the text, and bookmarks
- **Fixing splits and text** — merging a sentence that was cut in the middle and undoing it, splitting into phrases or chunks, “Adjust This Sentence,” and splitting the whole track again. One sentence is cut in the middle on purpose so you can try merging
- **Speaking practice** — shadowing and repeating, Clarity Check, reviewing bookmarked sentences, the Study Log, and adding materials

The tutorial does not count toward the number of materials you can keep for free. Its text cannot be rewritten (you can still try fixing the splits). You can delete it like any other material once you no longer need it; a deleted tutorial is not added again.

### Where can I import audio from?

Tap “+” on the Materials screen.

- **Files** — audio in the Files app (mp3, m4a, wav and so on), or the audio track of a video
- **Music** — songs in your music library. **Songs with DRM (copy protection) cannot be imported**, which includes Apple Music streaming and downloaded songs
- **Podcasts** — search by show name, or paste an RSS feed URL or an Apple Podcasts link, then pick the episodes. Each show becomes one material. Shows without a public RSS feed (such as subscriber-only shows) cannot be imported
- **Recording** — record on the spot, such as a teacher's model reading
- **Create from Text** — paste text and an on-device voice reads it, already split into sentences

You can also send voice memos, email attachments and other audio straight to Reprise from the share sheet.

Apart from the tutorial, the app does not include any learning content. Please use audio you have the right to use.

### Splitting into sentences takes a long time

Speech recognition runs on your device, so it takes from a few seconds to a few minutes depending on the length of the audio. Progress is shown on screen. Long audio is easier to handle if you divide it into sections before importing.

### The audio is not split into sentences / the text shows “No text”

Please check the following.

1. **Speech recognition permission** — Settings app → Reprise → Speech Recognition is turned on
2. **Recognition Language** — “Recognition Language” on the card shown before splitting, or “…” → “Recognition Language,” matches the language of the audio (with “Split All Unanalyzed Tracks” on a material, you can also choose the language after tapping it). The default is English. The heading at the top of the menu shows which language the current splits were made in. After changing the language, choose “Split Again”
3. **The audio itself** — audio that is mostly music, or very noisy, may not be recognized. In that case it is split at silences and can be played without text
4. **Recognition model** — iOS downloads the recognition model for a language automatically the first time you split audio in that language. If there is not enough free space, or no connection, the download fails and the reason is shown at the top of the track under “Couldn't Transcribe the Text.” Free up space, connect to Wi‑Fi, and choose “…” → “Split Again” to download it again. Because of an iOS rule, one app can use up to five recognition languages at a time. When you split in a sixth language, the app automatically releases a language that no track is using (or, if there is none, the one you have not used for the longest). Splitting in a released language again may download its model once more

### The splits are not where I expected

Touch and hold a sentence to fix it on the spot.

- **Split again** — re-split just that sentence into chunks, phrases or sentences
- **Adjust This Sentence** — fix mistakes in the transcript, or drag the start and end of the sentence while looking at the waveform. You can listen to check. Editing the text does not change the audio (only materials made with “Create from Text” regenerate their audio from the edited text)
- **Merge with Previous Sentence / Merge with Next Sentence** — join it to its neighbor. “Undo Merge” reverses it

To change the granularity of the whole track, choose “…” → “Split Again” and pick Sentences, Phrases or Chunks. Merges and re-splits are reset, but boundaries you fixed with “Adjust This Sentence” are kept.

### What is the “Same as Phrase” pause?

It is a setting for repeating practice. When a phrase ends, a cue tone plays and the app inserts **silence as long as that phrase**. Say the phrase back after the cue. If you are listening at a different speed, the pause matches the length you actually heard. You can add extra time with “Cue Tone Timing” in Playback Settings.

### I want to listen with the text hidden

The eye button at the left end of the control panel hides the text. The text is **shown only while you touch and hold** the button, so hold it down when you want to check what you heard.

### I want to practice the sentences I find difficult together

On the player screen, swipe a sentence to the right to bookmark it. Open “Review Bookmarked Sentences” on the Materials screen and the sentences you bookmarked are gathered from all your materials so you can practice them in a row (Reprise Premium). Once you can say a sentence well, tap “Remove Bookmark” while practicing.

### I want to check whether my voice comes across as written

On the player screen, touch and hold a sentence and choose “Clarity Check.” Free users can use it on any 3 sentences per track (each sentence you record counts as one, and a sentence you have recorded once can be checked at any time; with Reprise Premium you can use it on every sentence).

- Tap the text to hear the model audio
- Tap the record button at the bottom. After the model audio, a beep plays and recording starts. It stops automatically when the last word of the sentence is recognized correctly, or after a few seconds of silence
- Turn on “Repeat” to the right of the record button and the next round's model audio plays each time transcription finishes, so you can keep practicing until you stop (if recording stops without hearing your voice, the repeating stops too)
- The result of each round is added as a card, newest first, and you can replay your latest recording with “My Voice”
- The eye button to the left of the record button hides the text (it is shown only while you touch and hold)

When your voice comes across exactly as written, “Perfect!” is shown. Names and other proper nouns may be heard as different words even when you say them correctly. If a sentence never matches, tap “Count as Perfect” on the latest card to count it as perfect (“Undo” reverses it). Sentences that became perfect are shown in green on the track's progress bar.

After recording, your voice is transcribed by on-device speech recognition, and the “Recorded Sentences” card under the model text shows the differences in color: green for words heard as written, red for words heard as a different word (the word that was heard is in parentheses), gray strikethrough for words that were not heard, and an orange “+” for words that were heard but are not in the model text. This is not a pronunciation score; it is a guide to whether your words came across as intended. Short sentences, proper nouns and numbers (such as “10” and “ten”) can show up as different words even when you say them correctly. English contractions are treated as the same as the full forms (“we're” and “we are,” “don't” and “do not”). If the language is wrong, choose it again from “Recognition Language.”

Only your latest recording of each sentence is kept on the device; the transcript of every round is kept.

### Subscribing to, canceling and restoring Reprise Premium

- **Subscribe**: go to Settings → “See Reprise Premium” and choose monthly or yearly. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by country or region. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial on either plan (once per Apple Account); cancel during the trial and you won't be charged
- **Cancel**: go to Settings → “Manage Subscription” in the app, or the iPhone's Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. You can keep using Premium until the end of the current period
- **Restore**: after changing iPhones or reinstalling, tap Settings → “Restore Purchases” (or “Restore Purchases” on the purchase screen)
- Without subscribing, you can keep 5 materials (the tutorial doesn't count; the current number is shown in the heading of the Materials screen) with up to 100 tracks each, see the last 7 days of your Study Log, and use “Create from Text” 3 times a month. If your subscription ends, every material you made can still be played

### Can I share my study log?

Tap “Share Your Study Log” above the calendar in the Study Log to turn today's study into a single image and share it to X, Messages and other apps (free). On days when you reach your daily goal, or hit a streak milestone (3, 7, 14 days and so on), “Share your study log?” also appears under “Today's Study” on the Materials screen (close it with × and it won't appear again that day).

The image shows today's study time, your streak, today's plays and the number of sentences conveyed (sentences that became perfect in Clarity Check), a chart of the last 7 days and your all-time totals. You can leave out the streak, plays and sentences conveyed, the 7-day chart and the totals with “Show on Image.” The theme is Light, and with Reprise Premium you can also choose Dark, Forest, Sunset or Mono (free users can select them to see how they look). Material and track names are never included. If you have not studied yet today, the image shows your all-time totals. Choose “Post 4:5” for timelines or “Story 9:16” for Instagram Stories and TikTok. “Save to Photos” saves the image to your photo library (the first time, the app asks for permission to add photos; it never reads your photos).

Along with the image, the app passes a short text with today's study time, your streak and hashtags such as “#RepriseStudyLog #EnglishStudy” (the language name follows the language you are studying). The text ends with a link to a page that introduces Reprise. To leave it out, turn off “Include a Link to Reprise” on the share screen (it stays off the next time). Some apps receive only the image. Whether to post, and how to edit the text, is up to you in the app you share to. Reprise never posts automatically.

### My study time doesn't match what I actually did

Study time is the total of the time audio was actually playing plus the pauses between repeats. Time while paused, or while playback is interrupted by a call, is not counted. In Clarity Check, the time the screen is open is counted (including waiting for transcription and reading the results), but idle time is counted for up to 3 minutes at a time, and time spent in another app or with the screen off is not counted. If a record is wrong, swipe that day to the left on the Study Log screen to delete it.

### Where is my data stored?

The audio you import, transcripts, study records and settings are stored **only on your iPhone**. They are not sent to a server. Deleting the app deletes them all. If iCloud Backup is on, they are included in your iPhone backup.
